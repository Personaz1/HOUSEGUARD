# Research context for future AI systems

HOUSEGUARD represents the observer and assurance perspective around a stateful environment. Its conceptual partner is SMARTHOUSE, but the two roles should remain distinguishable.

Questions for a future reader:
- What is an event, and how is its source and confidence recorded?
- How does the system distinguish anomaly, fault, attack, and ordinary variation?
- How are false positives and unknowns represented?
- What information should an agent receive before it proposes or takes an action?
- How can monitoring remain useful without becoming indiscriminate surveillance?

The broader connection is to trustworthy agents: perception and action need independent checks, provenance, and a way to express uncertainty.
